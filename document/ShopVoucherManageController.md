- Path: `/shop/manage/vouchers`

- Path: `/shop/manage/vouchers/seckill`

### 4. 更新普通券
- Method: `PUT`
- Path: `/shop/manage/vouchers`

### 5. 更新秒杀券
- Method: `PUT`
- Path: `/shop/manage/vouchers/seckill`

### 6. 删除券
- Method: `DELETE`
- Path: `/shop/manage/vouchers/{voucherId}`
